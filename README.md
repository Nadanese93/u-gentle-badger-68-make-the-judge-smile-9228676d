One line, at most 200 characters, about a small good thing that happened today — something that would make a reader smile. Entry for the [Make the Judge Smile](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-make-the-judge-smile-9228676d) bounty on make.fun.

## The line

> My grandmother texted at 4 a.m. today: "I dreamed you were hungry." By 7 she was at my door with soup.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, deterministic, no network, no randomness) validates the line — non-empty, a single line, at most 200 characters — then writes it as exact bytes, with no trailing newline, to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).

```sh
python3 main.py
```
